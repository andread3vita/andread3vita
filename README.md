## 🚀 About Me

I am an experimental physicist interested in understanding the universe through its most fundamental constituents and interactions.

My fascination with physics began in high school, when I discovered that the behaviour of the universe could be described through particles and a small set of fundamental interactions. Since then, I have pursued this interest through my studies and research, with the goal of investigating these phenomena firsthand.

I believe that one of the most valuable ways to contribute to experimental physics is to explore alternatives to state-of-the-art instruments and reconstruction techniques. For this reason, my interests lie at the intersection of:

* 🔬 **Experimental High-Energy Physics**
* 🧲 **Particle-detector research and development**
* 🛤️ **Charged-particle tracking and vertex reconstruction**
* 🤖 **Machine learning for physics**
* 🌐 **Future collider experiments**

I am particularly interested in the **Future Circular Collider**, as future experiments will require a new generation of detectors and reconstruction algorithms capable of meeting unprecedented precision and performance requirements.

## 🔭 Current Research

### PhD in Physics — CERN & University of Padua

I am a PhD candidate at **CERN** and the **University of Padua**, working within the **EP-FCC group** on tracking reconstruction for the **FCC-ee**.

My research focuses on the use of **drift chambers** for high-precision charged-particle reconstruction. The reconstruction pipeline includes:

```text
Detector hits
     │
     ▼
Track finding
     │
     ▼
Track vertexing
     │
     ▼
Vertex finding
     │
     ▼
Vertex fitting
```

My work aims to develop and evaluate reconstruction strategies suitable for the demanding physics programme of a future electron–positron collider.

I am also a member of **RD_FCC Italy**, which conducts research and development on detector technologies for FCC-ee.
